# Privacy Policy

**Hospio360 — Hotel Management Platform**

**Last Updated: March 23, 2026**

**Effective Date: March 23, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hospio360 ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our hotel management platform ("Platform").

This policy applies to all users — hotel owners, managers, staff, and individuals whose data is processed through the Platform (such as hotel guests).

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account details** — name, email, phone number, password, position
- **Business details** — business name, registration number, type, address, tax/VAT numbers, website, timezone, currency
- **Financial details** — bank account information, payment transactions, payout records
- **Guest details** (entered by hotel staff) — guest name, contact info, booking dates, room assignments, payment records, corporate affiliation
- **Operational data** — inventory records, orders (restaurant, laundry, rental), purchase orders, lost & found items, financial records
- **Communications** — chat messages, support tickets, feedback

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Technical data** — IP address, device info, browser type, login timestamps
- **Activity data** — audit logs tracking staff actions (who did what, and when)
- **Security data** — login attempts, failed logins, session tokens

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Paystack** — payment verification and transaction status
- **SendGrid** — email delivery status
- **Infobip** — WhatsApp message delivery status
- **Cloudinary** — file upload metadata

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Running the Platform** — processing bookings, payments, inventory, orders, reports, and all hotel operations
- **Managing accounts** — authentication, roles, permissions, billing
- **Communicating with you** — sending booking confirmations, payment receipts, order updates via email, WhatsApp, and in-app notifications
- **Keeping things secure** — monitoring logins, rate limiting, fraud detection, audit logging
- **Improving the Platform** — analytics, performance monitoring, feature improvements
- **Legal obligations** — compliance with laws, responding to legal requests

---

## 4. How We Share Your Information

**We do not sell your personal information.**

We only share data with:

- **Paystack** — to process payments, bank transfers, and payouts
- **SendGrid** — to deliver emails (confirmations, receipts, OTPs)
- **Infobip** — to send WhatsApp notifications
- **Cloudinary** — to store uploaded files and images
- **Within your organization** — hotel owners, managers, and staff can see data relevant to their role and permissions
- **Legal authorities** — when required by law, court order, or to prevent fraud

In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred to the new entity with prior notice.

---

## 5. Data Security

We protect your data with:

- Password hashing and TLS/SSL encryption in transit
- JWT-based authentication with token expiration
- Role-based access control across 10 staff roles
- Login rate limiting and account lockout
- OTP verification for password resets
- IP monitoring and comprehensive audit logging
- Regular data backups

---

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active accounts** — data is kept while your account is active
- **Terminated accounts** — data is deleted after 90 days, unless required by law
- **Payment and financial records** — retained as required by tax and financial regulations
- **Guest data** — retained while the hotel's account is active
- **Notifications** — periodically cleaned up automatically

---

## 7. Your Rights

You can:

- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** inaccurate information (or update via profile settings)
- **Delete** your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Export** your data in CSV, Excel, or PDF formats
- **Opt out** of marketing communications at any time
- **Object** to certain types of data processing

To exercise these rights, contact us via the in-app support system or email at privacy@hospio360.com. We will respond within 30 days.

---

## 8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies to maintain your login session and Platform functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them will prevent you from using the Platform.

---

## 9. Guest Data

Hotels are responsible for their guests' data. As a hotel using Hospio360:

- **You** are the data controller — you decide what guest data to collect and why.
- **We** are the data processor — we store and process guest data on your behalf.
- **You** are responsible for obtaining guest consent before collecting their data or sending them notifications.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 11. International Data Transfers

If you access the Platform from outside Nigeria, your data may be transferred to and stored in Nigeria. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

## 12. NDPR Compliance

In accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA):

- We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently.
- We collect data only for specified, legitimate purposes.
- We report data breaches to the relevant authority within 72 hours.
- You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data as outlined in Section 7.

## 13. Data Breach Notification

If a data breach affects your information, we will:

- Notify affected users within 72 hours.
- Report to the relevant data protection authority.
- Explain what happened, what data was affected, and what steps we are taking.
