## 2. Description of Service

Hospio360 is a cloud-based hotel management platform that provides the following services:

- **Property Management**: Hotel registration, room management, and guest booking operations.
- **Front Desk Operations**: Check-in/check-out management, guest registry, and reservation handling.
- **Financial Management**: Revenue tracking, expenditure management, financial reporting, and analytics.
- **Payment Processing**: Online payment collection via integrated payment gateways, payment link generation, bank transfer processing, and POS terminal support.
- **Inventory & Procurement**: Stock management, supplier management, purchase orders, goods receipt, and stock movement tracking.
- **Restaurant & Bar Management**: Food item management, order processing, kitchen sales tracking, and bar inventory management.
- **Laundry & Valet Services**: Laundry order management, item pricing, and payment tracking.
- **Rental & Guest Services**: Equipment and item rental management, maintenance tracking, and customer management.
- **Staff Management**: Employee onboarding, role assignment, permission management, and activity logging.
- **Corporate/Organization Management**: Organization-linked bookings, corporate billing, and spending cap management.
- **Communication**: In-app messaging, email notifications, WhatsApp notifications, and real-time chat.
- **Reporting & Export**: Revenue reports, consumption reports, financial analytics, and data export in CSV, Excel, and PDF formats.
- **Lost & Found Management**: Item tracking, claiming, and status management.
